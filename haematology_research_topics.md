# Clinical Haematology Research Topics

Topics for autonomous generation of case series, systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

**Notes for the agent framework**
- Feasibility rankings are judgement-based and unverified. Before starting any topic, check PubMed, Europe PMC and PROSPERO for duplicates.
- Reporting standards: **CARE** for case series, **PRISMA 2020** for systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
- Topic IDs (#) are the same in both lists.
- Removed as already published: clonal haematopoiesis in solid tumours; epigenetic mutations in ALL prognosis.

---

## Part 1: Master List (All Topics)

### Case Series

1. **Toxicities of T-cell redirecting therapies (CAR-T, bispecific antibodies):** opportunistic infections, ICAHT/delayed cytopenias, severe CRS/ICANS, HLH-like features
2. **Long-term toxicities of targeted agents:** dasatinib pleural effusions and PARP inhibitor-associated t-MDS/AML (latency)
3. **Hyperhemolysis syndrome in sickle cell disease** treated with tocilizumab
4. **Hemoglobinopathies and enzymopathies from regional registries:** Malaysia Thalassemia Registry, G6PD variants and primaquine hemolysis
5. **Secondary HLH in infection:** severe dengue and EBV-related HLH
6. **Opportunistic infections in haematology patients** (e.g., chronic disseminated candidiasis in acute leukaemia)
7. **Unusual presentations:** synchronous malignancies (e.g., granulocytic sarcoma with gallbladder adenocarcinoma) and haematological manifestations of rare systemic diseases (anaemia revealing B-cell malignancy, necrobiotic xanthogranuloma with myeloma)
8. **Bone marrow examination series** on the aetiology of pancytopenia
9. **Rare or emerging molecular leukaemia subtypes:** RUNX1-MECOM, IDH2-mutated AML, p190 BCR-ABL B-ALL
10. **Richter syndrome in CLL:** ibrutinib-responsive or sustained-remission cases
11. **Rare haemostatic disorders and hereditary thrombocytopenias** (e.g., GNE-related)
12. **VEXAS syndrome:** cytopenias, MDS overlap, JAK inhibitors, azacitidine and transplant outcomes
13. **Novel therapies in refractory autoimmune cytopenias:** CAR-T (AIHA, ITP, APS) and complement inhibitors such as iptacopan (AIHA, cold agglutinin disease)

### Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses

14. **Caplacizumab in acquired TTP**, including TPE-free regimens
15. **Complement inhibitors in PNH (network meta-analysis):** crovalimab, eculizumab, ravulizumab, pegcetacoplan
16. **Targeted therapies in myeloproliferative neoplasms:** rusfertide in polycythemia vera and momelotinib in myelofibrosis
17. **Luspatercept in lower-risk MDS** by mutational background
18. **Venetoclax plus HMAs in AML** by molecular subgroup (IDH1/2, FLT3, NPM1, TP53)
19. **CAR-T in autoimmune haematologic disease** (ITP, AIHA), pooled proportions
20. **Newer ITP agents** (rilzabrutinib, efgartigimod, TPO-RAs), network meta-analysis
21. **Post-transplant cyclophosphamide in non-malignant diseases** (sickle cell, thalassaemia, aplastic anaemia)
22. **Clinical gene therapy and gene editing in sickle cell and thalassaemia**
23. **Supportive care in anaemia:** ESAs in cancer-related anaemia (including gemtuzumab ozogamicin in AML) and restrictive vs liberal transfusion thresholds in haemato-oncology
24. **Haematological adverse events of immune checkpoint inhibitors** (network meta-analysis)
25. **Metagenomic NGS for infections** in haematology/oncology patients
26. **Haematologic malignancies and COVID-19**, and long-term effects of disease-modifying therapies
27. **Prognostic factors and machine-learning models** for haematologic cancer outcomes (including CHIP and atherothrombosis)
