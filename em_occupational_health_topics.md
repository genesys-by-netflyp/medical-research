# Emergency Medicine & Occupational Health Research Topics

Topics for autonomous generation of case series, systematic reviews and meta-analyses at the
intersection of **emergency medicine** and **occupational health**. Each topic is filed under
both specialties.

**Notes for the agent framework**
- Feasibility was verified empirically (2026-10-01) via PubMed E-utilities + Europe PMC
  full-access gate for case-series synthesis (≥80% of cohort retrievable full text;
  no-go if ≤1 source passes). See per-topic feasibility below; re-verify at start time.
- Reporting standard: **CARE** for case series, **PRISMA 2020** for SR/MA.
- Worker scope is deliberately **broadened** (all workers/users, not gig/app-based only):
  a gig-worker restriction was verified as a dead end (≤2 relevant case publications).
- Non-English sources (Chinese, Japanese, etc.) are IN SCOPE and must be analysed, not excluded.

## Case Series Topics (verified 2026-10-01)

### Topic E1 — Acute pesticide poisoning / atypical toxidromes in agricultural workers
- **Specialties:** Emergency Medicine · Occupational Health
- **Proposed title:** *Acute pesticide poisoning in agricultural workers: an aggregated
  case-series synthesis of atypical toxidromes, prehospital presentation and outcomes*
- **Feasibility: SUFFICIENT (best candidate).** ~80 case-type publications; top-25 sample
  100% open access (PMC). Includes novel agents (indoxacarb, avermectin, novel herbicides/
  fungicides) and classic organophosphate/paraquat presentations. Multi-case OA sources exist.

### Topic E2 — Exertional heat stroke in outdoor occupational settings
- **Specialties:** Emergency Medicine · Occupational Health
- **Proposed title:** *Exertional heat stroke in outdoor occupational settings: a
  literature-based case series of presentation, prehospital cooling and outcomes*
- **Feasibility: SUFFICIENT (modest cohort).** ~10 genuine occupational EHS case reports/
  series with full text (aluminium smelter worker, fatal foudroyant MODS, field-cooling case
  study). Expect Japanese/Chinese-language sources. Epidemiological surveillance excluded.

### Topic E3 — Micro-mobility injury patterns (broadened: all e-scooter/e-bike users)
- **Specialties:** Emergency Medicine · Occupational Health (delivery/courier riders included)
- **Proposed title:** *Injury patterns in electric scooter and e-bike users: an aggregated
  case-series synthesis of published case series*
- **Feasibility: VIABLE WITH CAVEAT.** 24 case-type publications but only 11/24 (46%)
  confirmed full text; pivotal series (foot/ankle, paediatric, dental) paywalled. Feasible as
  an ~11–13-source OA series; below the 80% gate — requires user decision or PDF procurement.
- **Do NOT** scope to app-based/gig workers (only 2 relevant publications).

### Topic E4 — Lithium-ion battery thermal runaway injuries (broadened: all device users)
- **Specialties:** Emergency Medicine · Occupational Health (couriers, warehouse, retail)
- **Proposed title:** *Burns, blast and inhalation injury from lithium-ion battery thermal
  runaway in micro-mobility and personal-device users: a literature-based case series*
- **Feasibility: VIABLE WITH CAVEAT.** ~21 case-type hits, ~12 full text, incl. a PMC
  e-scooter battery detonation case series; pivotal HF-inhalation and thermal-runaway burn
  cases paywalled. Borderline — same caveat as E3.

## No-Go topics (verified 2026-10-01 — do NOT revive without new evidence)
- **3D-printing emission inhalation injury:** 0 genuine clinical case reports (exposure-measurement literature only).
- **Solar PV installation electrocution:** 0 case reports specific to solar workers.
- **Suspension trauma in wind-turbine/telecom technicians:** ~2 genuine clinical cases, 1–2 full text (rest are reviews/guidelines/volunteer experiments).
- **Paramedic injury from post-naloxone patient violence:** 0 clinical case reports (survey/epidemiology territory only).
