# Project: mNGS for infections in haemato-oncology patients (topic #25)

## One-paragraph summary
Metagenomic next-generation sequencing (mNGS) is increasingly used to diagnose infections in patients with haematologic malignancies or after HSCT/CAR-T, populations in which immunosuppression blunts classic signs and conventional microbiology is slow or insensitive. This project will systematically review and meta-analyse the diagnostic performance (and/or yield) of mNGS in haemato-oncology populations, estimating pooled sensitivity and specificity against conventional diagnostics/clinical diagnosis where 2x2 data allow, and pooled incremental diagnostic yield where they do not.

## PICO / case definition
- Population: Patients with haematologic malignancies, aplastic anaemia, HSCT or CAR-T recipients, or haemato-oncology patients with neutropenia/immunocompromise presenting with suspected infection
- Intervention / Exposure: mNGS of any specimen type (blood/plasma/serum, BAL/respiratory, CSF, tissue, stool)
- Comparator: Conventional microbiological diagnostics (culture, serology, PCR, antigen tests, histopathology) and/or final clinical diagnosis at discharge/follow-up
- Outcomes (primary / secondary):
  - Primary: pooled diagnostic yield (proportion of mNGS tests with positive result identified as pathogen) and/or pooled sensitivity & specificity (bivariate model) where 2x2 tables exist
  - Secondary: pooled positivity rate by specimen type, turnaround-time comparison, pathogen spectrum (bacteria/virus/fungus/parasite), effect on therapy change

## Inclusion criteria
- Primary study (cohort, case-control, RCT) evaluating mNGS in haemato-oncology/HSCT/CAR-T/neutropenic patients with extractable per-patient or aggregate 2x2 or yield data
- Any specimen type; any commercial or in-house mNGS platform
- Full text retrievable (PMC/OA); peer-reviewed
- Language: any language the pipeline can analyse (non-English sources extracted in source language)

## Exclusion criteria
- Case reports with <5 patients (noted but excluded from pooled analysis; may feed narrative)
- mNGS in solid tumour/transplant (non-haemato) populations without haematologic subset
- Reviews, editorials, consensus statements, cost studies without primary diagnostic data
- Duplicate/cohort-overlapping publications (largest series kept)
