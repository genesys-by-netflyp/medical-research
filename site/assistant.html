FAQ

Is this allowed by journals?

Most major publishers (ICMJE member journals, Elsevier, Springer Nature, Wiley) permit AI-assisted preparation of manuscripts with disclosure, while prohibiting AI authorship and AI-generated fabricated content. Our service is built around exactly that line: disclosed assistance, human verification, human authorship and responsibility. Always check your specific target journal's policy before submission.

How long does a paper take?

Case series typically 3–7 days in the queue and production; SR/MA longer depending on literature volume. You see live status in your dashboard.

What if my topic can't be done honestly?

We reject it and refund your credit — with a short feasibility report explaining exactly which sources lack full-text access.

Is my submission private?

Yes. Assistant deliverables are private to your account. They only become public if and when you publish them.

Who owns the output?

You do. The drafts are delivered to you for you to verify, edit, and submit as your own work — with the AI assistance disclosed.
