# Project: Acute pesticide poisoning in agricultural workers — aggregated case-series synthesis

## One-paragraph summary
Acute pesticide poisoning remains a major occupational emergency, and novel agrochemicals
(strobilurin fungicides, neonicotinoids, indoxacarb, avermectins, novel herbicides) are
producing toxidromes unfamiliar to prehospital and emergency clinicians. This project
synthesises published case reports and case series (all languages the agent can analyse)
into an aggregated structured case series describing agent classes, exposure circumstances,
prehospital/ED presentation, management and outcomes in agricultural workers, with emphasis
on atypical or emerging toxidromes. CARE-compliant, AI-assisted, human-reviewed at the end.

## PICO / case definition
- Population: Agricultural workers (farmers, pesticide applicators, farm labourers, crop
  workers) of any age with acute poisoning from agricultural chemicals (insecticides,
  herbicides, fungicides, rodenticides, fumigants). Broadened scope: not restricted to any
  employment arrangement.
- Intervention / Exposure: Acute exposure to agricultural pesticides (occupational or
  occupational-context; mixed/suicidal exposures included if agent is agricultural).
- Comparator: N/A (case-series synthesis); descriptive comparison across agent classes.
- Outcomes (primary / secondary):
  - Primary: clinical toxidrome at prehospital/ED presentation (categorical, per agent class)
  - Secondary: time to presentation, decontamination, antidote use, ICU admission,
    complications, mortality, long-term sequelae (e.g. organophosphate-induced delayed neuropathy)

## Inclusion criteria
- Published case report or case series (any journal, any language analysable by the agent)
  with individual-patient or per-case data on acute agricultural pesticide poisoning
- Explicitly agricultural/occupational exposure context (or farm-suicide poisoning with an
  agricultural agent, flagged as a subcohort)
- ≥1 documented clinical parameter beyond bare outcome (e.g. symptoms, signs, labs, treatment)

## Exclusion criteria
- Pure epidemiological/surveillance studies without case-level clinical detail
- Animal or in-vitro studies
- Reviews/case compilations without extractable per-case data (may be mined for their
  referenced primary cases)
- Non-agricultural exposures (industrial, household-only) unless the agent is predominantly
  agricultural
- Chronic low-level exposure without an acute presentation
